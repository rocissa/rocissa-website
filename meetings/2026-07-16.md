## Special Presentation:<br>Non-Human Identities: The Cloud's Biggest Blind Spot

Most organizations have more machines talking to each other than people--service accounts, API keys, AI agents, OAuth tokens--and most teams have zero visibility into what they're doing or what they can access. We'll talk through why most cloud breaches start with an overprivileged non-human identity, what "zero-standing privilege" actually looks like in practice, and how to start cleaning things up.
